Google Business Profile 101

July 2026 · 4 min read · Full Plate Marketing Co.

For a lot of local businesses, your Google Business Profile is the first thing a customer sees. Not your website. Not your social. The little box that shows up when someone searches your name or "plumber near me."

If you have ever searched your own business on Google and seen the panel with your hours, photos, and reviews, that is your Google Business Profile. You may still know it by its old name, Google My Business. Same tool, new name.

Here is why it matters. When someone searches for what you do, Google decides who to show first. A complete, active profile tells Google you are open, real, and worth showing. A half-finished one gets skipped.

The basics worth getting right

  • Claim it. If you have never logged in, your profile may already exist and be sitting unclaimed. Claiming it puts you in control.
  • Complete every field. Hours, phone, website, service area, categories. Empty fields cost you.
  • Add real photos. Your storefront, your team, your work. Profiles with photos get more clicks than ones without.
  • Pick the right categories. This is how Google matches you to searches. Be specific.
  • Keep your hours current. Nothing loses a customer faster than driving to a "closed" sign when your profile said open.

Two quick wins most owners miss

  • Reviews. Ask happy customers to leave one, and reply to every review you get. Google notices activity, and so do the people reading them.
  • Posts. You can post updates, offers, and news right on your profile. Most local businesses never do. That is your opening.

You do not need to do all of this in one sitting. Even claiming your profile and filling it out completely puts you ahead of most of the businesses around you.
