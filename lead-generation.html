Frequently asked questions

Why doesn't my business show up on Google Maps?

Google ranks local results on three things: relevance, distance and prominence. In plain terms, how well your profile matches the search, how close you are, and how well known you are online. Wrong categories, a thin profile, few reviews and listings that don't match are the most common problems, and all of them can be fixed.

How long until I see more calls?

Profile fixes can show up within a few weeks. Local search and AI visibility usually build over two to six months. Paid ads can bring calls within days of launching. That's why we often start ads while the free channels grow underneath them.

Can you guarantee a first-page ranking?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. Google decides rankings, and results change based on where the person searching is standing. What we can promise is the work: a complete profile, fixed listings, steady reviews and honest monthly reporting on where you stand.

Do I keep my Google Business Profile if I stop working with you?

Yes. Your profile belongs to you. We work on it as a manager while you stay the owner. If we ever part ways, you keep everything, including your reviews, photos and history.

Can you remove a bad review?

Only if it breaks Google's policies, like spam, a fake account or content that has nothing to do with your business. In those cases we can flag it. For honest negative reviews, the best move is a calm, helpful reply. Future customers read your reply as closely as the review.

What counts as a lead, and how will I know you brought it in?

A lead is a call, form, message or booking from someone interested in what you sell. We use call tracking and form tracking so each one shows where it came from, whether that was Google Maps, a search result, an ad or social media.

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your season, your goals and how competitive your category is. Many local businesses start with a modest monthly budget, see what it brings in, and grow from there. Your ad budget is paid to Google or Meta directly and is separate from our fee.

Do I still need ads if I rank well on Google?

Not always. If Google Maps and search already bring in steady calls, ads may only make sense before busy season or when you launch something new. We will tell you honestly if ads won't pay off for your business.

How do I show up in ChatGPT and other AI answers?

AI tools lean on many of the same signals as local search: a complete Google profile, real reviews, a clear website and mentions on other trusted sites. We check what these tools say about your business today and work on the gaps.

Do you buy or sell lead lists?

No. Bought lists are strangers who never asked to hear from you, and contacting them can create legal headaches. We focus on people who are already looking for what you do.
