Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 27, 2026. We keep this simple. Here is what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you fill out our contact form, we collect what you choose to share: your first name, last name, email address, phone number, business name, website, industry, and anything you tell us about your goals. We do not collect payment information on this website.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to you, set up your free scorecard, and provide the services you ask about. We do not sell your information to anyone. Ever.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels, and we do not run analytics that build a profile of you. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your device's IP address in order to deliver the font. That is standard across most of the web, and we do not use it to track you.

How long we keep it

We keep your contact information as long as we need it to respond to you and provide our services, or until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information you have shared. Email us at hello@fullplatemarketing.co and we will take care of it. You can opt out of our emails at any time.

Children

Our website and services are meant for business owners. They are not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top.

Contact us

Questions about your privacy? Email hello@fullplatemarketing.co. We are proudly based on the Outer Banks of North Carolina.
