The pantry

Free tools we actually recommend.

We get asked all the time what tools are worth using. Here are five that are genuinely free to start and genuinely useful. No fluff.

Google Search Console

Free from Google

Shows you what people search to find you and how your site shows up. The clearest window into your online visibility, and most owners have never opened it.

Google Gemini

Free from Google

An AI assistant for writing emails, brainstorming, and answering quick questions. Think of it as a helper that never sleeps.

Canva

Free to start

Design tool for social posts, flyers, and menus. No design experience needed. If you can drag and drop, you can use it.

Notion

Free to start

An all-in-one spot for notes, plans, and keeping your business organized in one place instead of scattered across sticky notes.

Slack

Free to start

Team messaging that keeps work chatter out of your text messages. Handy the moment you have more than one person to coordinate with.

Tools help, but they only go so far on their own. If you want a second set of eyes on which ones actually fit your business, that is what a Kitchen Check is for.
