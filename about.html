Why we started

Marketing got messy. It chased the dollar and forgot the customer. Too many companies lead with a shiny solution before they even understand the problem, then sell business owners a pitch instead of a partnership. That is marketing built backwards. Full Plate exists to do it right: diagnosis before prescription, the right solutions in the right order, and the customer experience at the center of everything. Because underneath it all, a great local business started with someone who fell in love with a craft, and they deserve a partner who protects that, not one more salesman chasing a close.

The experience behind it

Full Plate is built on more than fifteen years of marketing and operations experience inside some of the largest companies in the world. The systems that run enterprise marketing are the same ones that quietly transform a local business. Most small businesses just never get access to them.

We bring that enterprise-level thinking down to the local level, in plain language, at a pace that makes sense for where your business actually is.

Meet your server

Hi, I'm James Kahle, founder of Full Plate Marketing Co. I live and work right here in Kill Devil Hills.

I've been blessed to lead marketing, operations, and executive teams at some of the world's largest companies, including Amazon, Walmart, CVS, and Chipotle. I've also helped smaller companies like Argyle Interactive, Thrive Financial, and EcoGen America build their marketing systems from scratch and scale to seven figures and beyond. Most recently, I was Director of Marketing Operations for Kitty Hawk Kites, running marketing across its family of brands and roughly 30 shops.

I genuinely enjoy helping teams organize and scale their marketing. There is a widening gap between what AI and automation can do and what busy owners actually have time to implement. I close that gap with education, clear communication, and simple systems, because better processes mean more predictable revenue and a better experience for everyone who touches the business.

Today I work with service businesses across Dare and Currituck counties, and Full Plate is a proud member of the Outer Banks Chamber of Commerce. Connect with me on LinkedIn.
